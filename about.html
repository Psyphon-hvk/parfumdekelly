THE BEGINNING

A dream distilled
into every drop

Parfum De Kelly was born in 2023 from a single obsession — to create fragrances that feel like memories. Kelly, our founder, spent years studying the ancient craft of perfumery across Paris, Grasse, and Marrakech before returning to Nairobi with a vision unlike anything the continent had seen.

Each fragrance in our collection is a chapter of her journey — a blend of African warmth, French precision, and Middle Eastern opulence. We source only the rarest raw materials: Bulgarian rose absolutes, Cambodian oud, Madagascan vanilla, and hand-harvested Kenyan neroli.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Philosophy

01

Authentic Craft

Every bottle is hand-blended in small batches, never mass-produced. We believe perfumery is a living art — patient, intuitive, and deeply personal.

02

Rare Ingredients

We travel the world to source the finest raw materials — from the oud forests of Southeast Asia to the rose fields of Grasse — sparing no effort for excellence.

03

Timeless Design

Beauty extends beyond the scent. Our bottles are designed as objects of desire — minimal, architectural, worthy of the vanity they will grace for years.

04

We are proudly rooted in Africa. Our scents carry the warmth of the savanna, the richness of the soil, and the vibrancy of a continent in full bloom.

05

Luxury should never come at the cost of our planet. We are committed to sustainable sourcing, cruelty-free formulations, and eco-conscious packaging.

06

HOW WE CREATE

The Art of Making

I

Sourcing the Finest

Our journey begins at the source. We travel to the world's most prestigious growing regions — rose fields in Grasse, oud forests in Laos, neroli groves in Kenya — selecting only the top 5% of each harvest by hand.

II

The Alchemy of Blending

Kelly personally composes every fragrance accord. Using traditional maceration and cold enfleurage alongside modern techniques, each formula undergoes months of refinement — sometimes over 200 iterations — before it is deemed worthy.

III

Maceration & Maturation

Like a fine wine, our fragrances are aged. The blended concentrate macerates in alcohol for a minimum of six weeks, allowing the notes to marry and the full character of the scent to emerge with depth and balance.

IV

Handcrafted Bottling

Each bottle is hand-filled, sealed, and inspected individually. The labels are letterpress-printed on 400gsm cotton stock. No two bottles are identical — each carries a subtle uniqueness that reflects the human hands that made it.

THE VISIONARY

Meet Kelly

FOUNDER & MASTER PERFUMER

Kelly grew up surrounded by the botanical richness of the Kenyan highlands — jasmine climbing garden walls, eucalyptus on the morning air, the deep earth after rain. These sensory memories became the language she would spend her life translating into fragrance.

"A perfume is a conversation between who you are and who you wish to become."

After training under master perfumers in Grasse and building a decade of expertise, she returned home with one mission: to create luxury fragrances rooted in African identity, crafted for the world.
